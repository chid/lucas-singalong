Age ranges are editorial starting points, not prerequisites or milestones. For a new language, begin with easy songs at any age. Mandarin uses Simplified Chinese with Pinyin; Cantonese uses Traditional Chinese with Jyutping. Romanisation helps a grown-up follow along; listen to the recordings for pronunciation. Start with a chorus, follow his interests, and revisit the richer words later. Some songs work well long before or after these ranges.

A LITTLE NOTE FOR GROWN-UPS

Let the song spill into play.

Keep a favourite in the mix. Try one new song beside a song he already loves. Repeat the ones he chooses.

Give the words something to do. Scoop sand, row a cushion boat, stomp through a pretend jungle. Follow his ideas.

Leave a little space. Pause before a familiar word and give him a chance to join in. Singing, pointing and moving all count.
